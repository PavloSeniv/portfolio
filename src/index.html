Get to know me!

Hey! I'm Pavlo Seniv, a Full-Stack Web Developer based in Ivano-Frankivsk, Ukraine ♥. I design and build fast, accessible and maintainable products for the web — from e-commerce storefronts to enterprise dashboards.

My daily stack is React, Next.js and TypeScript on the front end, backed by Node.js, Strapi and Laravel. I care about clean architecture, thoughtful UI/UX and shipping things that actually work in production.

I've delivered remote projects for real businesses and love turning complex requirements into simple, polished interfaces. Feel free to reach out — I'm always open to a good product challenge.
